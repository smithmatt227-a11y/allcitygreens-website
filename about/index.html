About

Cincinnati's daily price guide, run by someone who actually shops here.

All City Greens is an independent, free daily email that tracks deals and prices across Cincinnati-area dispensaries. We're not a dispensary. We don't sell anything. We just monitor public menus every morning and pass the best of it along.

Why this exists

Cannabis pricing in Ohio is a moving target. The same product can swing $15 in a day. Dispensaries run flash sales that aren't advertised anywhere except their own menu. Driving past three shops to compare prices burns more gas than the discount you'd save. So I built a scraper that does it for me each morning — and once I'd done that, it felt obvious to share.

How it works

Every morning before sunrise, an automated script visits the public menu of each tracked dispensary, pulls live pricing, deduplicates products across sister locations, and ranks the best deals by category. The daily email lands in your inbox each morning. The website updates at the same time so you can browse from your phone in the parking lot.

All data comes from each dispensary's own public website — no scraping behind a login, no insider feeds, no special access. If a dispensary updates their menu before you visit, you'll see the latest the next morning.

What we don't do

  • Take money from dispensaries. No sponsored placements, no "featured deal" payola, no affiliate kickbacks. The deal ordering is purely by discount.
  • Sell your data. Email is for the daily newsletter. Full stop. See the privacy policy.
  • Run ads. Site stays clean. Email stays clean.
  • Guarantee prices. Menus change throughout the day — always confirm at the dispensary before you roll out.

Who's behind this

All City Greens is a one-person side project run out of Cincinnati. Questions, corrections, or "you missed this dispensary" notes go to hello@allcitygreens.com and I'll see them.
