FAQ

Common questions

Everything people usually want to know about how All City Greens works.

How often are prices updated?
Every morning. Our system automatically checks menus across every tracked dispensary before your alarm goes off.
Are these prices guaranteed to be accurate?
Prices are pulled directly from each dispensary's online menu. They're accurate at time of collection but can change throughout the day — always confirm before visiting.
Is All City Greens affiliated with any dispensary?
No. We're completely independent and not paid by, sponsored by, or affiliated with any dispensary we cover. We just track public prices.
Is the newsletter really free?
Yes, always. No paid tiers, no ads in the email. Just the daily price guide.
Do you cover medical patients?
Yes. The newsletter includes a separate section for deals available to medical card holders, clearly labeled so rec-only visitors can skip it.
