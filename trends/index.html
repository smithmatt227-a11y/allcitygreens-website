How Cincinnati cannabis prices move.

Daily averages tracked since March 2026. The bold line is the citywide median flower price — click any dispensary to see how it sits against that. Nothing fancy, just the data we already scrape every morning, smoothed over seven days so you're looking at the trend rather than the noise.

Flower prices across Cincinnati

7-day average, dollars per item. Names sorted cheapest first.

About the data. Each morning's scrape pulls every flower SKU from each dispensary's public menu and records the average price. Sister locations under the same brand (e.g., The Garden at Camp Washington + Sycamore) are merged into a single line. Days with fewer than 5 SKUs for a dispensary are excluded as unreliable signal. Every line is a seven-day rolling average, and a day is only drawn where at least three scrapes landed in the week before it — so a stretch the scraper missed shows up as a gap rather than a straight line pretending to be a trend. A dispensary that has reported nothing for 30 days is dropped from the chart entirely instead of being carried forward at a stale price.
